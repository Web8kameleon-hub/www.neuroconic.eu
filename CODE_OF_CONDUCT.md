# Neurosonic Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the
Neurosonic community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

### Expected Behavior
- **Truth First**: Always provide verified information (No Fake)
- **Respect**: Treat others with dignity and respect
- **Collaboration**: Help others grow and learn
- **Transparency**: Be open about your work and intentions
- **Accountability**: Take responsibility for your actions

### Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment, discrimination, or hate speech
- Deliberate spread of false or misleading information (Fake)
- Sharing private information without consent
- Disruptive or disrespectful behavior
- Any violation of the Neurosonic Constitution

## Enforcement

Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in:
1. Warning
2. Temporary ban from the community
3. Permanent ban from the community
4. Legal action (if applicable under German/EU law)
